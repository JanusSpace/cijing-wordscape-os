# Community Card Package Takedown Policy

Janus Wordscape OS may support community card packages as user-created content.
Community packages are treated as untrusted input and as creator-owned content.

The project does not claim ownership of community packages and does not
guarantee that community packages are free of third-party rights issues.

## Creator Responsibility

Creators who publish or submit a card package should ensure that they have the
right to share every included item, including:

- text definitions and explanations;
- examples and translations;
- source excerpts;
- audio files and pronunciation data;
- images and screenshots;
- tags, metadata, and derived datasets.

Each shared package should include:

- creator or maintainer name;
- source list and citations;
- package license;
- commercial-use permission status;
- contact or issue-reporting method.

## Rights Holder Reports

If you believe a card package, source citation, audio asset, image, or related
content infringes your rights, please provide:

- the content or package name;
- the specific item or file path;
- the right you claim is infringed;
- proof of ownership or authorization;
- your contact information;
- the requested action.

Reports can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker or another
official contact channel listed by the project owner.

## Project Response

When a credible report is received, the project may:

- remove links to the reported package;
- remove hosted copies from official repositories or release assets;
- mark the package as disputed;
- request clarification from the uploader or maintainer;
- restore content if the report is resolved.

The project may act before a final legal determination when continued hosting
would create obvious risk.

## No False Official Status

Community card packages may not present themselves as official Janus Wordscape
OS packages unless they are explicitly approved by the project owner.

## No General Content Monitoring Promise

The project may provide safety checks for package format, URL protocols, audio
URLs, file size, and schema validity. These checks do not guarantee copyright
compliance.

This file is not legal advice. Review the final policy with a qualified
professional before operating a public package marketplace.
