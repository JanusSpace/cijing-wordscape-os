# Commercial Use Policy

Janus Wordscape OS follows an Anki-like open community model:

```text
Software code: open under AGPL, commercial use allowed with open-source reciprocity.
Official brand: reserved.
Official card packages and content assets: commercial reuse reserved.
Community card packages: owned and licensed by their creators.
```

## Software Code

The software source code is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

You may use the code commercially, including in a company, school, or internal
tool, as long as you comply with AGPL.

If you distribute a modified version or provide a modified version as a network
service, you must provide the corresponding source code under AGPL-compatible
terms.

## Brand and Official Identity

Commercial use of `雅努斯词境 OS`, `Janus Wordscape OS`, related names, logos,
icons, screenshots, and visual marks requires written permission unless the use
is a truthful compatibility or attribution statement.

See `BRAND_POLICY.md`.

## Official Card Packages and Content Assets

Official word-card packages, sample packs, production blueprints, field
standards, official screenshots, manuals, and official explanation assets are
not commercially reusable without written permission.

You may inspect and use official sample content for personal learning,
evaluation, and non-commercial demonstration. You may not sell, repackage, or
bulk-redistribute official content as a paid product or paid service without a
separate commercial license.

See `CONTENT_LICENSE.md` and `DOCS_LICENSE.md`.

## Community Card Packages

Community card packages belong to their creators or rights holders.

Janus Wordscape OS does not automatically own community packages, and the
project does not guarantee that a community package is safe for commercial use.

Before using a community package commercially, check its package metadata,
license, sources, and author permissions.

If no license is declared, treat the package as all-rights-reserved by its
creator.

## Third-Party Algorithms and Dependencies

Third-party dependencies remain governed by their own licenses. This project
uses `ts-fsrs` under MIT and records its scheduler version for auditability.

See `NOTICE` and `docs/THIRD_PARTY_ALGORITHM_GOVERNANCE.md`.

## Summary

Allowed without separate permission:

- commercial use of unmodified or compliant modified AGPL software code;
- internal company or school usage of the app;
- truthful attribution such as "uses Janus Wordscape OS source code under AGPL";
- personal and non-commercial evaluation of official sample content.

Requires written permission:

- selling official card packages or official content assets;
- using the official brand for a commercial distribution;
- bundling official content into paid courses, paid apps, or paid services;
- bulk-copying official content into another vocabulary platform;
- using official content assets for commercial model training.

This file is not legal advice. Review the final licensing strategy with a
qualified professional before commercial rollout.
